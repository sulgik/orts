# 2020 material

The synthetic runner and archived plot outputs behind Kim and Kim (2020,
arXiv:2003.01905): `simul/simulate.py`, its text outputs (`0_*.txt`,
`02_*.txt`: no drift and common drift, for Beta-TS, Full-TS and OR-TS), the
two figures, and the notebook that drove them. They predate the registered
experiments of the 2026 paper and are kept as an illustration, not as
evidence. The runner has been pointed at the `orts` package but is otherwise
unchanged.
